# Reference answer for the judged checklist

1. Read every diagnosis, follow-up and normal-marrow tube under /data with FlowIO, name each fluorescence channel by its $PnS marker, and compensate its fluorescence values with the file's own SPILL matrix; put fluorescence on an arcsinh scale and scatter on a linear scale.
2. Learn white-cell, CD34-positive and CD117-positive white-cell gates from the laboratory's pre-gating of every diagnosis tube and of half of the healthy donors, so follow-up tubes, which carry no gating, get the same gates.
3. Rebuild each reported LAIP on its diagnosis tube from the laboratory's LAIP report: inside its primary compartment (CD34-positive or CD117-positive white cells when the phenotype names them, all white cells for a monocytic phenotype, the blast compartment otherwise), place each named marker against the healthy donors' distribution of the same compartment, and take the box whose diagnosis event count best matches the reported LAIP size.
4. Train one classifier per LAIP on its diagnosis events against the rest of its diagnosis tube and the healthy donors' marrow, and raise its threshold until the held-out healthy donors put at most 1e-5 of their white cells inside it, so normal and regenerating CD34-positive and CD117-positive progenitors stay outside the LAIP.
5. In each follow-up tube, track every LAIP of the case whose markers the tube's panel measures, inside the LAIP's primary compartment, and count its events among the CD45-expressing white cells left after debris and doublets are excluded.
6. Take a tube's MRD as its largest single LAIP over its white cells times 100, the top LAIP as that LAIP (the lower number on a tie), and a case's MRD as its largest tube; call the case MRD positive at 0.1 percent or more and negative below 0.1 percent.
7. Mark a tube evaluable for negativity when it holds more than 500,000 white cells and the compartment assessed for its best-tracked LAIP holds at least 100 events.
8. Write the four tables and the masks from the same event masks, so every number recomputes from them, and write method.md with the same case calls and MRD values as patients.csv.

A correct method file states that each file's own SPILL matrix compensated the fluorescence values before any gate was drawn. It defines each LAIP from the laboratory's diagnosis pre-gating and LAIP report, and it limits each LAIP gate with the healthy donors' marrow before applying it to follow-up tubes, so normal and regenerating progenitors are not counted as leukaemic cells. It tracks every LAIP whose markers a follow-up tube measures, divides by the CD45-expressing white cells left after debris and doublets are excluded rather than by all recorded events, takes the largest single LAIP per tube and the largest tube per case, calls MRD positive at 0.1 percent or more, and applies the 500,000 white-cell and 100-event compartment thresholds for negativity. Its stated calls and MRD values equal those in patients.csv. It computes every value from the files, never from a published or remembered value, leaves every file under /data unchanged, claims no check its numbers contradict, and never reports a plain blast-gate or CD34-positive gate count as a case's MRD.
